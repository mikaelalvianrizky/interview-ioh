# Chatbot self-assessment

The answers themselves are in `chatbot_answers.md`, generated by `python chatbot.py --eval`.
Questions 1 to 4 are the required ones, 5 to 9 are mine, chosen to find weak spots.

| # | Question | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Berapa harga paket 10GB yang masa aktifnya 30 hari? | Correct | Lists Freedom Internet 10GB (Rp 25.000) and IMClass Belajar 15GB, whose general quota is also 10 GB for 30 days. | 2 | What happens if I don't recharge for 60 days? | Correct, with noise | The right FAQ section (3) comes first. The second passage (Refunds, score 0.59 against 0.60) is irrelevant. | 3 | How many subscribers recharged last month? | Correct | 3,029 for August 2026, taken from the database, matching the sum of the August rows in Q2. The period is stated in the answer. |
| 4 | Who is the CEO of Indosat? | Correct | Declined: no topic word in the question, and the top score (0.33) is below the 0.35 threshold. |
| 5 | Can I get a refund if I activated a package by mistake? | Correct | Refunds section first, and it contains the exact rule ("Packages are non-refundable once activated, including packages activated by mistake"). Second passage (Auto-renew) is only loosely related. |
| 6 | Berapa harga paket roaming untuk umrah? | Correct, poorly presented | Roaming Umrah 8GB (Rp 175.000) comes first. The question is in Indonesian but the passage text stays in English, and the second passage (Roaming Global) is noise. |

## Harder questions

The nine questions above made the 0.35 threshold look safe. Nineteen harder ones (slang, and off-topic
questions that sound like telco) showed it is not: "ada esim ga?" scored 0.11, "harga iPhone 15 berapa?"
scored 0.43. I added Indonesian aliases per FAQ section and a topic-word check. Results are in the
README and `tests/test_retrieval.py`. Still wrong on the 20 held-out questions:

|---|---|---|
| paket nelpon yang paling murah apa? | FAQ 6 (activation) first | No price filter |
| rekomendasiin dong paket internet yang paling terjangkau | Freedom Internet 8GB first, the cheapest is Yellow Harian 1GB | No price filter |
| XL lagi ada promo apa? | Answered with the Promotional messages section | "promo" is a topic word, so competitor questions get through |

| beli yellow harian 2x sehari, kuota yang pertama hilang? | Yellow Harian 1GB first, the stacking rule is in FAQ 6 | The package name pulls its catalog chunk to the top |

## Patterns behind the failures

- Embeddings are weak on numbers and constraints (GB, days, price). The keyword filter covers GB and days written as digits, nothing else.
- The bot always shows the top two passages, even when the second one is unrelated.
- Data questions are detected with keyword rules. A phrasing outside those rules falls through to retrieval and gets declined. That is safe (no invented number) but unhelpful.
- Without a generation step there is no translation and no summarising, so Indonesian questions get English passages.
- Aliases are whack-a-mole: each one helps the phrasing it was written for and can pull other questions
  to the wrong section ("cara isi pulsa" beats "transfer pulsa" for "bisa transfer pulsa ke temen?").
- The topic-word list cuts both ways: telco words in a non-telco question get through, and an English
  question with no listed word is declined.
