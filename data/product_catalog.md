# Prepaid Product Catalog — 2026

All prices in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR), inclusive of tax. Quota and validity apply
from the moment of activation. Prices are for the prepaid segment only and may
differ from postpaid equivalents.

*This is a fictional catalog created for a technical assessment. It does not
describe any real commercial product.*

---

## Family: Freedom U (Unlimited tiers)

Unlimited data with a daily fair-usage threshold. After the threshold is reached,
speed is throttled until midnight local time.

### Freedom U Basic
- **Price:** Rp 50.000
- **Quota:** Unlimited, FUP 1.5 GB/day
- **Validity:** 30 days
- **Throttle speed:** 384 Kbps

### Freedom U Plus
- **Price:** Rp 100.000
- **Quota:** Unlimited, FUP 3 GB/day
- **Validity:** 30 days
- **Throttle speed:** 1 Mbps

### Freedom U Max
- **Price:** Rp 150.000
- **Quota:** Unlimited, FUP 5 GB/day
- **Validity:** 30 days
- **Throttle speed:** 2 Mbps
- **Notes:** Includes 200 off-net call minutes.

### Freedom U Ultra
- **Price:** Rp 300.000
- **Quota:** Unlimited, FUP 10 GB/day
- **Validity:** 30 days
- **Throttle speed:** 5 Mbps
- **Notes:** Priority network access during congestion. Hotspot tethering allowed.
