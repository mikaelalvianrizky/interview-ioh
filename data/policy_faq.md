# Prepaid Policy FAQ

Reference document for prepaid account rules: active period, grace period,
recharge, package handling, refunds and registration.

*This is a fictional policy document created for a technical assessment. It does
not describe the terms of any real operator.*

---

## 1. Active period (masa aktif)

Every prepaid number has an **active period**. While the active period is running,
the number can make calls, send SMS, use data and receive incoming calls.

Each recharge extends the active period. The extension depends on the recharge
amount, not on the package purchased:

| Recharge amount | Active period added |
|---|---|
| Rp 5.000 – Rp 9.999 | 7 days |
| Rp 10.000 – Rp 24.999 | 15 days |
| Rp 25.000 – Rp 49.999 | 30 days |
| Rp 50.000 – Rp 99.999 | 45 days |
| Rp 100.000 – Rp 199.999 | 60 days |
| Rp 200.000 and above | 90 days |

Extensions are cumulative up to a maximum active period of 365 days.

---

## 2. Grace period (masa tenggang)

When the active period ends, the number enters a **grace period of 30 days**.

During the grace period:
- The number can still **receive** calls and SMS.
- The number **cannot** make outgoing calls, send SMS, or use data.
- Any remaining main balance is preserved but cannot be spent.
- Active packages are suspended and their remaining quota is frozen.

A single recharge of any amount during the grace period restores the number to
the active state immediately, and any frozen package quota becomes usable again
for its remaining validity.

---

## 3. What happens if I do not recharge for a long time

The timeline after your last active day is as follows:

- **Day 1 to day 30 (grace period):** incoming only. The number is still yours.
- **Day 31 onwards:** the number leaves the grace period and is **permanently
  deactivated**. The main balance is forfeited, all packages are terminated, and
  the number is returned to the numbering pool.
- **Day 31 to day 60:** the number sits in a quarantine queue and is not yet
  reassigned. Recovery is possible only by visiting a service centre with the
  original registration documents, and is not guaranteed.
- **After day 60:** the number is recycled and may be reassigned to a new
  customer. Recovery is no longer possible under any circumstance.

In short: a customer who does not recharge for 60 days will normally have lost
the number permanently. There is no reactivation path after recycling.

---

## 4. Recharge channels

Recharges can be made through:
- The operator's mobile application
- Minimarket cashiers (Indomaret, Alfamart and equivalents)
- Retail outlets selling physical or electronic vouchers
- Mobile banking and ATM
- E-wallet applications
- UMB short code *123#

Channel does not affect the active period granted. Some channels charge a small
service fee which is collected by the channel, not the operator.

---

## 5. Physical voucher rules

- A physical voucher must be redeemed before its printed expiry date.
- Each voucher code can be redeemed exactly once.
- Entering an incorrect code five times in one hour locks voucher redemption on
  that number for 24 hours.
- Expired vouchers cannot be exchanged, refunded or extended.

---

## 6. Package activation and stacking

- Most data packages can be stacked. When two packages are active, quota from the
  package expiring soonest is consumed first.
- Daily packages such as Yellow Harian cannot be stacked. Purchasing a second one
  replaces the first, and the unused quota of the first is lost.
- Add-on packages require an active base package. If the base package expires, the
  add-on expires with it.
- A package purchased during the grace period activates only after the number
  returns to the active state.

---

## 7. Auto-renew

- Auto-renew is available on selected 30-day packages.
- Renewal is attempted on the expiry date. If the main balance is insufficient,
  the system retries once every 6 hours for 48 hours before cancelling.
- Auto-renew can be turned off at any time through the app or by dialling the
  package's UMB code. Turning it off does not refund the current period.
- A package that renews automatically does not extend the active period unless the
  renewal charge falls into one of the bands in section 1.

---

## 8. Fair usage policy (FUP)

- Unlimited packages apply a daily fair usage threshold. Beyond the threshold,
  speed is reduced until 00:00 local time, when the threshold resets.
- Throttled sessions are not charged against the main balance.
- Quota-based packages above 25 GB apply a weekly fair usage check. Exceeding
  20 GB within seven days may reduce speed for the remainder of that week.
- Using the connection for commercial redistribution, including operating a public
  hotspot for payment, is a breach of the fair usage policy and may result in
  suspension.

---

## 9. Refunds

- Prepaid balance is non-refundable once loaded, except where a recharge was
  duplicated by a system error.
- Duplicate-charge claims must be raised within 30 days of the transaction, with
  the transaction reference from the channel.
- Approved refunds are returned as prepaid balance, not as cash or bank transfer.
- Packages are non-refundable once activated, including packages activated by
  mistake. There is no cooling-off period.
- Roaming packages are refundable only if the customer did not attach to any
  foreign network during the validity period.

---

## 10. Balance transfer

- Balance can be transferred between prepaid numbers on the same network.
- Minimum transfer is Rp 5.000, maximum is Rp 200.000 per transaction.
- Maximum five transfers per day per sending number.
- A sending number must have been active for at least 180 days.
- The transferred amount does not extend the receiving number's active period.

---

## 11. Registration

- Every prepaid number must be registered with a national identity number and
  family card number before activation.
- A single identity number may register a maximum of three prepaid numbers on one
  operator.
- Registration can be completed through the app, UMB, or at a service centre.
- Unregistered numbers are blocked from outgoing service 30 days after first
  activation.

---

## 12. Number ownership transfer

- Ownership transfer requires both parties to attend a service centre in person
  with original identity documents.
- Numbers in grace period or quarantine cannot be transferred.
- Outstanding auto-renew subscriptions are cancelled on transfer.

---

## 13. Lost or damaged SIM replacement

- SIM replacement preserves the number, balance, active period and active packages.
- Replacement requires the registered identity document.
- A replacement fee applies and is deducted from the main balance where possible.
- Service is restored within two hours of replacement in most cases.

---

## 14. eSIM

- eSIM is available on the same terms as physical SIM.
- One number can hold only one active eSIM profile at a time.
- Moving a profile between devices requires re-issuing the QR code.
- Roaming packages work identically on eSIM and physical SIM.

---

## 15. Network priority during congestion

- During congestion, traffic is prioritised in this order: emergency services,
  premium unlimited tiers, quota-based packages, throttled sessions.
- Priority does not guarantee a minimum speed.

---

## 16. Data usage measurement

- Data usage is measured in binary units, where 1 GB equals 1024 MB.
- Usage is counted at the network layer and includes protocol overhead, so figures
  may differ slightly from the device's own counter.
- Sessions are rounded up to the nearest 1 KB.

---

## 17. Complaint handling

- Complaints can be raised through the app, call centre, or a service centre.
- The standard resolution target is 3 working days for billing disputes and
  7 working days for network quality issues.
- A complaint reference number is issued at the point of submission and is
  required for any follow-up.

---

## 18. Promotional messages

- Customers may opt out of promotional SMS through the app or by replying STOP.
- Opting out does not stop service notifications such as quota alerts, package
  expiry warnings or grace period reminders.
- Opt-out preferences take effect within 48 hours.

---

## 19. Quota alerts

- Alerts are sent automatically at 80% and 100% of package quota consumption.
- Active period expiry reminders are sent 3 days and 1 day before expiry.
- Grace period entry generates an immediate notification, followed by reminders on
  day 7, day 20 and day 28 of the grace period.

---

## 20. Service suspension for policy breach

- Suspected fraud, bulk SMS abuse, or fair usage breach can result in immediate
  suspension.
- Suspended numbers retain their balance and active period during investigation.
- Investigation outcome is communicated within 14 days.
- Reinstated numbers have their active period extended by the number of days lost.
